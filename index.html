The Gauntlet.
Challenge I · 6 Sentences · Stand & Answer
One question, every time: which word or phrase is the finite commander?
01
Challenge I · The Gauntlet
The committee appointed to investigate the allegations has not yet published its findings.
Find the commander — the finite verb or verb phrase.
The Soldier (bait)
appointed
The Commander (tense)
has … published
appointed is a reduced relative — who was appointed. It only describes the committee. The time of the sentence lives in has (not yet) published.
02
Challenge I · The Gauntlet
Studies examining the long-term effects of sleep deprivation reveal a sharp decline in cognitive performance.
Find the commander — the finite verb or verb phrase.
The Soldier (bait)
examining
The Commander (tense)
reveal
examining is a verbal — that examine. It describes the studies. The tense-carrying commander is reveal, agreeing with Studies.
03
Challenge I · The Gauntlet
Having reviewed the evidence presented by both sides, the tribunal reached a unanimous verdict.
Find the commander — the finite verb or verb phrase.
Two Soldiers (bait)
Having reviewed · presented
The Commander (tense)
reached
Two soldiers in disguise. Having reviewed = perfect participle (a setup). presented = reduced relative (that was presented). Only reached carries tense.
04
Challenge I · The Gauntlet
The strategy proposed by the new administration to curb inflation remains deeply controversial.
Find the commander — the finite verb or verb phrase.
Two Soldiers (bait)
proposed · to curb
The Commander (tense)
remains
proposed describes the strategy. to curb states purpose. Neither rules. remains is the throne — the finite verb.
05
Challenge I · The Gauntlet
Researchers must reconsider the assumptions underlying the original model.
Find the commander — the finite verb or verb phrase.
Soldiers (bait)
reconsider · underlying
The Commander (tense)
must reconsider
reconsider alone has no finite power. The finite modal auxiliary is must, and the full finite verb phrase is must reconsider. And underlying is a soldier describing the assumptions. A verbal can serve a verb phrase — never command alone.
06
Challenge I · The Gauntlet
To understand the crisis fully, economists analysing global trade patterns have proposed a new framework.
Find the commander — the finite verb or verb phrase.
Two Soldiers (bait)
To understand · analysing
The Commander (tense)
have proposed
To understand opens with purpose — not the verb. analysing describes the economists. The tense-carrying commander is have proposed.

B

The Signal
The sentence has two soldiers — conducted and working — but no commander yet. The blank must be the finite verb. having demonstrated, to demonstrate, demonstrating, to have demonstrated are all verbals — none can rule alone. Only demonstrate carries tense and agrees with experiments.

to reconsider
B

The Signal
make somebody DO something — the causative takes a bare verb, soldier #5. The tense already lives in made. So the blank cannot carry tense: not to reconsider, not reconsidering. Only the bare reconsider serves the phrase.
Challenge II · Question 03 · Translation
The rapid decline in biodiversity observed in tropical regions over the past three decades has prompted international organisations to develop more comprehensive conservation strategies.
A
Tropikal bölgelerde son otuz yılda gözlemlenen hızlı biyoçeşitlilik azalması, uluslararası kuruluşların daha kapsamlı koruma stratejileri geliştirmesini engelleyen bir faktör olmuştur.
B
Son otuz yılda tropikal bölgelerde yaşanan biyoçeşitlilik kaybı nedeniyle uluslararası kuruluşlar, koruma stratejilerini yeniden gözden geçirmek zorunda kalmıştır.
C
Tropikal bölgelerde gözlemlenen biyoçeşitlilik azalması, uluslararası kuruluşları son otuz yılda daha kapsamlı koruma stratejileri oluşturmaya yöneltmiştir.
D
Son otuz yılda tropikal bölgelerde gözlemlenen hızlı biyoçeşitlilik azalması, uluslararası kuruluşları daha kapsamlı koruma stratejileri geliştirmeye yöneltmiştir.
E
Tropikal bölgelerde otuz yıl içinde gözlemlenen biyoçeşitlilik kaybı, uluslararası kuruluşların oluşturduğu kapsamlı koruma stratejilerinin bir sonucudur.
Find the Commander First
Soldier: observed — it only describes the decline. Commander: has prompted → yöneltmiştir. Only one option preserves the subject (the rapid decline), the commander, the object (organisations), and the time frame in its right place — that is D.  A reverses meaning (engelleyen = prevented). B turns the subject into a reason (nedeniyle). C misplaces the time frame. E inverts cause and effect.

The Game.
Correct or Incorrect · 7 Sentences · Win an Ice Cream
Every sentence looks right. Not all of them are.
01
Correct or Incorrect?
Diana's bike is good transportation for her.
"Diana's" is a possessive modifier — not a noun. There is no noun "Diana" in the sentence. So "her" has no antecedent to refer back to. A possessive cannot serve as a pronoun referent in standard English. It must read: Diana's bike is good transportation for Diana.
02
Correct or Incorrect?
It is high time that Emre stopped postponing our ice cream treats.
"It is high time" requires the past subjunctive in the that-clause. "stopped" — not "stops" — is correct. The past tense here does not point to the past; it signals urgency: the action is overdue. Most students write "stops" and lose the mark.
03
Correct or Incorrect?
Painless, the operation took only ten minutes.
A fronted adjective modifying the subject — classical literary syntax. Full form: "The operation, which was painless, took only ten minutes." The adjective "Painless" correctly refers to "the operation." Some correct sentences look unusual because they are formal or literary — odd is not the same as wrong.
04
Correct or Incorrect?
Published ahead of schedule, the writer could sell thousands of copies of his novel.
Dangling participle. "Published" must refer to the subject of the main clause — but the subject is "the writer." The writer was not published. The novel was. Correct: "Published ahead of schedule, his novel sold thousands of copies." The thing published must be the subject.
05
Correct or Incorrect?
I would help the boy selling flowers were I you.
Type 2 Conditional — inverted, main clause first. Standard: "If I were you, I would help…" Advanced: "I would help… were I you" — the if-clause moves to the end, "if" is dropped, subject-auxiliary invert. Both the position and the inversion look wrong. Both are correct formal English.
06
Correct or Incorrect?
The journalist opened the folders, removed the papers, and threw them out.
Pronoun ambiguity. "Them" could refer to the folders or the papers — both grammatically valid. The reader assumes "papers" by proximity, but the sentence does not guarantee it. In standard written English an ambiguous pronoun reference is a structural error regardless of implied meaning.
07
Correct or Incorrect?
When Tuana ran into Zeynep in the street after a long time, she was overjoyed.
Pronoun ambiguity. "She" could refer to Tuana or Zeynep — both grammatically valid. We feel it means Tuana, but the sentence cannot confirm it. Who is happy here? The sentence does not know. That is the error.
